FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions answered

Foam wash kitna waqt leta hai?

Normal Sedan car ke liye lagbhag 25-30 minutes lagte hain. Isme exterior thick foam soak, pressure wash, aur microfiber towel se finish hand drying shamil hai.

Kya booking karwana zaroori hai ya direct aa sakte hain?

Aap direct wahn aakar bhi service le sakte hain. Lekin weekends par apna waqt bachane ke liye website ya phone se slot book karwana behtar rehta hai.

Kya SUV ya 7-Seater car ke charges alag hain?

Haan, SUV aur badi gadiyon ke liye normal rate se Rs 300 additional charges hote hain kyunki unki cleaning aur detailing mein ziada waqt lagta hai.
